Client Guide

Welcome! This guide shows you how to manage your guest list and wishes in your Google Sheet.

📋 TAB 1: RSVP (Guest List)

Every guest who RSVPs through the card appears here automatically.

  • How to add a guest manually (physical card):
    Scroll to the first empty row at the bottom. Type their Name, choose Yes/No for the dropdowns, and choose Manual for Source.
  • How to edit: Click the cell, type the new value, press Enter.
  • How to delete: Right-click the row number on the left, click Delete row.

💌 TAB 2: Wishes

Every wish from your guests appears here. This is private — only you can see it.

  • How to review a wish:
    Read the Wish column. Click the Status cell and choose from the dropdown:
  • Pending — Not reviewed yet.
  • Approved — Clean, keep it.
  • Rejected — Inappropriate, marked.
  • How to delete: Right-click the row number, click Delete row.

⚠️ IMPORTANT

Do NOT delete the header row (Row 1) or change the column names. Do not touch the first empty row where new RSVPs land.
